Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to P2P Mesh Chat ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our peer-to-peer chat application.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Google Sign-In to authenticate, we collect:

  • Display Name: Your chosen display name for the chat
  • Email Address: Your Google account email (used only for authentication and profile identification)
  • Profile Information: Basic profile data from your Google account (name)

We do not collect or store your messages on any central server. All chat messages are transmitted directly between users via peer-to-peer connections.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Create and maintain your user profile
  • Display your identity to other chat participants
  • Authenticate your access to the application
  • Enable you to create chat rooms (authenticated users only)

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage:

  • Authentication credentials (cached locally for convenience)
  • Chat history (stored only on your device)
  • User preferences and settings

We do not maintain any central database of user information or chat messages. Your data remains on your device and is not accessible to us.

5. Peer-to-Peer Communication

P2P Mesh Chat uses WebRTC technology for direct peer-to-peer communication:

  • Messages are encrypted end-to-end between participants
  • Communication occurs directly between users' devices
  • We use PeerJS for connection signaling, which temporarily routes connection metadata through their servers
  • No message content passes through or is stored by our servers

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is used exclusively within this application.

7. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

  • End-to-end encryption for all chat messages
  • Secure authentication via Google OAuth 2.0
  • Local data storage (reduces exposure to server breaches)

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your communications.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your local data (stored in your browser's localStorage)
  • Delete your local data by clearing your browser's localStorage
  • Revoke our access to your Google account via Google's security settings
  • Use the application as a guest without authentication

9. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the application or create an issue on our GitHub repository.
